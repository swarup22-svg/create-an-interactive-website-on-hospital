E-Library

An e-library (or digital library) is an organized online collection of digital resources—such as e-books, academic journals, research papers, and multimedia content—that users can access anytime and anywhere through computers, tablets, or smartphones. By removing physical boundaries and open-access or subscription-based constraints, it allows students, researchers, and general readers to search through massive databases with a few simple keywords. This virtual platform saves valuable time, lowers the cost of printed materials, and protects fragile documents from wear and tear while offering 24/7 learning opportunities.
